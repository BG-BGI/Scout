    UHF RFID —
      M7E Hecto EPC Gen2 reader (902–928 MHz, reads at range while driving). Scanning stays OFF until enabled; every read is pose-stamped and stored in the companion DB, which estimates each tag's map position from the drive-past geometry.

      RFID —
        Flipper Zero 125 kHz reader. Scanning stays OFF until enabled; every card read is stamped with map pose and stored in the companion DB.

        NFC —
          Flipper Zero 13.56 MHz reader (MIFARE/NTAG/ISO14443). Mutually exclusive with RFID — one radio at a time.
